# Review reports

- `device-report.txt`: the iPhone, its iOS version, the rootless environment, installed packages,
  processes and file paths.
- `trollrecorder-static-report.txt`: a static review performed on the Mac of the TrollRecorder app
  bundle, its plists, executables and signature entitlements.

These are local research outputs and are ignored by Git. They must be reviewed for device names,
usernames, serial numbers and local file paths before any public sharing.
